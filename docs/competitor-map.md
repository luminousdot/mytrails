# Competitor map

Race calendars runners in Serbia and the region use today, reviewed 27 Nov 2026 from their public pages. Update this file when a site changes or a new one turns up.

## At a glance

| Site | What it is | Coverage | Stand-out features | Business model |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [trkesrbija.rs](https://trkesrbija.rs/) | All-sport race calendar for Serbia | 231 events: trail (109), road (101), OCR, triathlon, nordic walking | Calendar, map, online registration, **GPX tool** (analyse a route and its climb), **leagues** across a season, ⌘K search, distances with D+ on each listing, blog, race travel packages abroad | Ads ("this spot can be yours"), travel agency partner (Run Away Travels), registrations |
| [trka.rs](https://trka.rs/) (Race center) | Registration platform with a public race list | Serbia plus a few abroad; 30+ sport types | **List and map side by side**, filter by race type and country, **registration status** (open, closed, opens on date), "Organize an event" for organisers, past events, language switch | Registration fees for organisers, ads |
| [mountain-race.rs](https://mountain-race.rs/kalendar-trka/) | Trail and mountain calendar for Serbia and the Balkans | Trail/mountain, Serbia first, also Montenegro, North Macedonia, Croatia | Filters by **series/league, country, distance range and climb range** (sliders), **ITRA-only and UTMB-stones-only** toggles, "in N days", GPX per race, ITRA points per distance, **results**, series pages, SR/EN, sign-in | Registrations, organiser services |
| [spisaktrka.com](https://www.spisaktrka.com/trke/kategorija/polumaraton?type=half_marathon) | Road-running calendar | Serbia and the region (Podgorica, Ljubljana, Sofia, Skopje) | **Category pages** (marathon, half, trail, ultra, 10K, 5K, relay) that read like landing pages, **"next edition expected in &lt;month&gt;"** for past races, organiser named on every card, services for organisers | Services for organisers |
| [runnmore.com](https://www.runnmore.com/kalendar-trka) | Running shop with a magazine and a calendar | Mostly road races in Serbia | Month grid calendar plus a **planner** view; part of a shop (shoes, clothing, brands) | Retail; the calendar brings shoppers |

## What it means for My Trails

**Everyone covers Serbia, and everyone is in Serbian.** The strongest generalist is trkesrbija.rs: it already has a map, a GPX tool and leagues. My Trails cannot win on "a calendar with a map". It wins on the things in PRODUCT.md that nobody combines:

- **Trail and mountain only, across the whole region**, not all sports in one country.
- **Course depth on every race page**: elevation profile, every distance, aid stations, ITRA points.
- **Organiser-first data**: organisers publish and own their page; others mostly aggregate or sell registrations.

**Gaps none of them fill well:** aid stations, a finish-time estimate per course, a fuel plan (Trail Station), race pages that read well on a phone in the field.

**Signals to act on:**

1. **Serbian language is table stakes.** All five sites are in Serbian (mountain-race.rs and trka.rs also in English). PRODUCT.md lists Serbian as undecided; this pushes it towards yes.
2. **Registration status matters to runners.** trka.rs and mountain-race.rs show open, closed or "opens on" dates. We only link out.
3. **Dates are known earlier elsewhere.** trka.rs already lists 2027 dates for WST races (Jablanik 25 Apr, Gradac Vidra 12 Jun, Sova Night 10 Jul, Bobija 14 Aug, Divča 25 Sep, Povlen 17 Oct) that mountain-race.rs has not published yet. Worth adding as a source when the demo data is unfrozen.
4. **"Next edition expected"** (spisaktrka.com) answers the empty-season problem we hit with WST.

## Feature ideas taken from these sites

|---|---|---|
| Registration status and the date it opens, on cards and race pages | trka.rs, mountain-race.rs | High; organisers can enter it in our form |
| "Next edition expected in &lt;month&gt;" for races without a new date | spisaktrka.com | High; solves the WST gap |
| Distance and climb as range sliders instead of fixed buckets | mountain-race.rs | Medium; buckets are simpler on a phone |
| UTMB stones filter next to ITRA points | mountain-race.rs | Medium; needs the data |
| Standalone GPX tool: drop any GPX, see the profile and climb | trkesrbija.rs | Medium; we already have the parser |
| List and map side by side on desktop | trka.rs | Medium; we switch views instead |
| Leagues and series standings | trkesrbija.rs, mountain-race.rs | High; already next after organiser tools |
| Results per race | mountain-race.rs | High; confirmed need |
| Category landing pages (ultra, sky, by country or region) for search | spisaktrka.com | Medium; good for discovery and SEO |
| Global search (⌘K) | trkesrbija.rs | Low |
